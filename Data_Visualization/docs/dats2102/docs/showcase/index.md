# Student Project Showcase

Final projects from **DATS 2102 Data Visualization for Data Science**. Each student chose a research question that mattered to them and built a complete, reproducible data story: data collection and cleaning, analysis, interactive visualization, and a written narrative, published as a Quarto report. Projects are shown with the authors' permission.

Some students publish their work on their own GitHub Pages sites; those are linked directly. Others are hosted here.

## Spring 2026

### Hosted on students' own sites

- **[How NFL Teams Approach Fourth Down (2016–2025)](https://matthewwolfgwu.github.io/fourth_down/trends.html)** — Matthew Wolf
  A decade of NFL play-by-play data (nflverse + FTN participation data): the league's go-for-it rate nearly doubled from 12.6% to 23.2%. Trend charts, breakdowns by quarter, score and distance, a team aggressiveness-vs-success quadrant plot, and a gradient-boosting model that predicts go/no-go decisions with ~92% test accuracy.

- **[Provider Access and Health Outcomes Across US Counties](https://adibahal.github.io/dats2102-final-project/FinalProject_Adi_Bahal.html)** — Adi Bahal
  Using the 2025 County Health Rankings for 2,795 counties, tests whether primary-care physician density predicts premature death and diabetes, stratifies by rurality, and maps regression residuals in interactive Plotly and Folium choropleths to show where counties do worse than access alone predicts.

- **[Why Does Railroad Safety Matter?](https://ashustef.github.io/DATS%202102%20Final%20Project.html)** — Anthony Shustef
  Federal Railroad Administration accident data (Form 54): accidents normalized by state area, train speed linked to equipment-damage cost via correlation heatmap and OLS regression, railroad comparisons with violin and bar charts, and Amtrak routes overlaid on a U.S. map with GeoPandas.

- **[Assessing Taylor Swift's Concert and Lyric Data](https://kjones912.github.io/datavizfinal/FinalProjectDataViz.html)** — Katy Jones
  445 concert records across five tours and 4,862 lyric lines from six albums: revenue-vs-tickets regression, interactive city rankings and country choropleths, plus a text-analysis arm with TF-IDF phrases, a Random Forest album classifier, and a critically read confusion matrix.

### Hosted here

- **[50 Cities, 365 Days: A Global Story of Weather, Climate, and Extremes in 2025](chow_global_weather_2025.html)** — Jia Hui Chow
  Daily weather across 50 major global cities in 2025, moving past simple "hot / cold / wet" labels to show how weather is actually experienced over time, with a machine learning model of climate profiles.

- **[Fragmented Fandom: Visualizing MLB Media Markets, TV Rights, Viewership, and Streaming Fragmentation](digiacomo_fragmented_fandom.html)** — Jonah DiGiacomo
  Follows the money, then follows the fan: as MLB finds more ways to monetize its games, how complicated and expensive does it become to actually watch? Includes a model of "watchability" across markets.

- **[The Great Fertility Decline](gregg_fertility_decline.html)** — Dillon Gregg
  A global data story on falling birth rates, 1960–2024: six decades of decline, where it is happening, crossing the replacement line, six country case studies, a multivariate view, and a small predictive model, with a limitations-and-ethics section.

- **[Stateless People in the World](sun_stateless_people.html)** — Miduo Sun
  The invisible population: global statelessness 2010–2022, where it is most concentrated, the South and Southeast Asia crisis (Rohingya in Myanmar, hill tribes in Thailand), and changes across ex-Soviet countries over time.

## Fall 2025

- **[Chasing the GOAT: NBA Greatness Analysis (1973–2025)](wong_nba_goat.html)** — Sky Wong
  A composite GOAT score across five decades of NBA players: trends by year, top vs. bottom players, a logistic regression on age with ROC curve, and GOAT scores by team location.

- **[Evolving Energy: Changes in the U.S. Energy Mix, 1949–2024](hernandez_evolving_energy.html)** — Jasmine Hernandez
  U.S. Energy Information Administration data on production and consumption by source since 1949: dot plots, slope charts, and indexed time series showing the shift from fossil-fuel dominance toward nuclear and renewables. [Source notebook and data](hernandez_evolving_energy_src/).
